Privacy Policy

Bakehop · Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy explains what Bakehop (the “App”) does with your information. The App is published by 1+1=2 LLC (“we”, “us”). It applies only to the Bakehop app for iPhone and iPad.

The short version

  • Bakehop collects no information about you or your child. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no accounts. We never ask for a name, email, or any other personal detail.
  • Your game is saved on your device only.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code.
  • The App has no links out of it, no chat, and no way to talk to other players.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple, behind a grown-up check. We never see payment details.

Information we do not collect

The App has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. We do not ask for or collect a name, email address, phone number, date of birth, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or location. We operate no servers for the App, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software. The App does not connect to the internet except through Apple, when a grown-up makes or restores a purchase.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game in its private storage on your iPhone or iPad, so it remembers where you left off. None of it is ever sent to us:

  • Your village: what you have built, your coins, your helpers, and where Clover is standing
  • Your recipe book, your regulars’ hearts and postcards, and the decorations you have placed
  • Settings, such as music, sounds, buzzes, tap-to-walk, and day and night
  • Whether you own the whole village

In-app purchase

“Open the whole village” is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and it can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. Before anything is bought or restored, the App asks a grown-up to answer a question a young child can’t. Apple processes the payment and tells the App only whether you own it. We never see your payment card, billing address, or Apple Account details. Purchases are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. To bring a purchase to a new device, open the village map, tap Orchard Hill, and choose Restore purchase.

Children

Bakehop is made for children, in the App Store’s Kids Category, and follows its rules. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The App has no ads, no analytics, no links out, and no way to contact other people, and every purchase sits behind a grown-up check. If you have a question about your child’s privacy, contact us at the address below.

Retention and deleting your data

We hold no data about you. To remove what the App stored on your device, delete the App, or choose Start a new game in its Settings (behind a grown-up check). Purchase records are kept by Apple and managed at privacy.apple.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information. Since we hold none, there is nothing for us to return or remove, but we are glad to answer any question, and you always have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The revised version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the App’s release notes.
