Privacy Policy

Booger & Stink · Last updated 30 September 2026

This policy explains what Booger & Stink (the “App”) does with your information. The App is published by 1+1=2 LLC (“we”, “us”). It applies only to the Booger & Stink iOS app.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, or any other personal detail.
  • Your heist in progress, your progress and your settings are stored on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress syncs through your own Apple Account. We cannot see it.
  • If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Apple’s Game Center.
  • There are no ads, and nothing in the App tracks you.
  • The App sends anonymous gameplay statistics to TelemetryDeck: how heists went, never who played them. We use them to tune how hard the game plays.
  • We run no crash-reporting service, and nothing we receive identifies you.

Information we do not collect

The App has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile of ours. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or precise or approximate location. We operate no servers of our own and embed no advertising or crash-reporting SDK. The one analytics service the App uses, and exactly what it receives, is described under Anonymous usage statistics.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game locally so it works and remembers where you left off. This data stays in the App’s private storage on your iPhone. Apart from the anonymous summary described under Anonymous usage statistics, none of it is sent to us:

  • A heist in progress, so Continue picks it up exactly where you left it
  • Your progress: the outfits and collars you have unlocked, the heists you have won, the snacks in Stuff Stink Ate, your best scores, and the achievements you have earned
  • Settings, such as sound, music and haptics, and which tips you have already seen
  • The outfit and collar you picked last
  • Whether you own the full heist

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the App keeps your progress (the unlocks, wins, album and best scores above, but not a heist in progress) in Apple’s iCloud key-value store, so it follows you to your other devices and survives reinstalling the App.

This data lives in your private iCloud storage, under your Apple Account. It is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it, no ability to read it, and no copy of it.

Sync is entirely optional. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn iCloud off for Booger & Stink in iOS Settings, the App works exactly the same way and keeps everything on the one device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App posts your scores to two leaderboards, Best Drop and Total Loot, and reports the achievements you earn. Game Center is Apple’s service: it shows your scores and achievements to you and, depending on your Game Center settings, to other players, under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center name or account. If you are not signed in, the App plays exactly the same.

Anonymous usage statistics

The App reports a few anonymous facts about how heists play to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built to avoid collecting personal data, run by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany. We use them to check that the game is neither too hard nor too easy for real players, and to see which parts of it people play.

When a heist ends, the App sends:

  • How it ended (busted, cashed out after beating Mr. Whiskers, or busted after keeping going), the room it ended in, and how close that room’s score came to its target
  • The outfit and collar, the Crew you had, the deals you took from Mr. Whiskers, and how many Poofs you used a room
  • Which of your heists it was (your first, your second, and so on), whether you own the full heist, and whether you are signed in to Game Center

When the full heist’s card opens, the App sends where it opened from and, in the Wardrobe, which outfit or collar you tapped; then how it went: bought, restored, cancelled, waiting for approval, or not gone through. Never a price, an order number, or anything about your Apple Account. When the story before your first heist ends, it sends whether you skipped it. Nothing else is reported: not your scores drop by drop, not your settings, not your Game Center name.

TelemetryDeck’s software adds basic technical details to each report: the App’s version, your iOS version and device model, screen size, time zone, language and region settings, and a few accessibility settings such as bold text. To count how many people play without knowing who they are, it also sends a code made by hashing (irreversibly scrambling) the identifier iOS assigns to our apps on your device. TelemetryDeck hashes it again when it arrives, so neither they nor we can recover the original. It is not an advertising identifier.

These statistics are not linked to your identity, not combined with data from other companies, and not used for advertising or tracking. We use them only in aggregate. TelemetryDeck’s handling of data is described in its privacy policy.

In-app purchase

The full heist is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and it can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. Apple processes the payment and tells the App only whether you own it. We never see your payment card, billing address, or Apple Account details. Purchases are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. Use Restore purchase on the full heist’s card, in the App’s Settings, to bring a purchase to a new device.

Children

Booger & Stink is made for a general audience. It has no accounts, no chat and no ads, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Retention and deleting your data

We hold no data that identifies you. The usage statistics are not linked to you, so there is no way for us to pick out one person’s records. To remove what exists:

  • On-device data: delete the App. Everything it stored locally goes with it.
  • iCloud data: in iOS Settings, open your Apple Account › iCloud, and turn off or remove Booger & Stink’s data. Deleting the App on all your devices and signing out of iCloud also clears it.
  • Game Center data: managed by Apple, in Settings › Game Center and at privacy.apple.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent, without being treated differently for it. Since we hold no personal information ourselves, most requests are best directed to Apple or TelemetryDeck, whose data the request actually concerns. We are glad to help you work out which, and you always have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Apple operates globally, so data it handles may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. TelemetryDeck is based in Germany. Each applies the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.
