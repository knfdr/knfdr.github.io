Privacy Policy

Magimo · Last updated 18 September 2026

This policy explains what Magimo (the “App”) does with your information. The App is published by 1+1=2 LLC (“we”, “us”). It applies only to the Magimo iOS app.

The short version

  • Magimo keeps a record of the theme-park moments you have done. To keep that record safe and bring it to a new phone, you sign in, and your record is stored in your account on Google Firebase.
  • What is stored is what you put in: the moments you mark done and when, the notes and photos you add, your trips, and the badges and titles you earn. Only you can see it. Magimo has no friends list, no feed and no public profiles.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your information.
  • Your location never leaves your phone. If you allow it, the App reads it only while you are using it, to show what is near you in a park and how far the next stop is. It is not stored and not sent to us.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it at any time.

Information you give us

Your account

You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password. Sign-in is run by Google Firebase Authentication. We receive:

  • your email address (with Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide it, in which case we receive a private relay address from Apple);
  • your name, if you enter one or your sign-in provider passes one on;
  • your sign-in provider's profile picture address, if it passes one on, so the App can show it on your own profile;
  • an account identifier that ties your record to you.

We never see or store your password. Firebase Authentication keeps it in hashed form, and password resets go through an email it sends you.

Your record

Everything below is saved to your account so that it is still there tomorrow and on your next phone:

  • the moments you mark done, with the date and time you chose;
  • the notes you write on a visit (up to 140 characters each);
  • the photos you add to a visit. The App makes a smaller copy and a thumbnail and uploads those; it does not upload your original, and it does not read the rest of your photo library;
  • your trips: dates, the parks for each day, your must-do list and the order you put it in;
  • the achievements, badges and titles you have earned, and the title you choose to wear;
  • your home park, if you set one;
  • whether you are a Supporter, so the App can unlock what that includes.

Please do not put anything in a note or a photo that you would not want stored. Notes and photos are yours; we do not read them, use them for anything else, or show them to anyone.

Information that stays on your device

  • Location. Magimo asks for location only after explaining why, and only “while using the app”. It is used on your phone to work out which park and land you are in, what is a short walk away, and the walking time to your next stop. It is never stored and never sent to us or anyone else. If you say no, the App works without it: you can tell it “I'm here” yourself. Tapping Directions hands the destination (not your location) to Apple Maps, which has its own policy.
  • Notifications. Trip and season reminders, and the optional nudges, are scheduled by the App on your phone. Nothing is sent to a notification server of ours, because we do not have one.
  • Camera and photos. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo for a visit. Choosing an existing photo uses Apple's picker, which hands the App only the photos you pick. Saving a card you made to your library uses add-only access: the App can add a picture, and cannot see your library.
  • Settings and small preferences, such as which reminders are on, which hints you have already seen, and a same-day “I'm here” check-in.

Information we do not collect

Magimo contains no advertising and no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your contacts, your precise or approximate location, your advertising identifier, your health data or your browsing history, and the App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Wait times, showtimes and park hours in the App come from our own database, which we fill from ThemeParks.wiki. Your phone reads them from us; it does not contact that service, and nothing about you is sent to it.

Sharing cards

When you share a postcard, a film strip or a badge card, the picture is made on your phone and goes only where you send it: Messages, your photo library, or the app you pick from the share sheet. We do not receive a copy. Whatever you post publicly is then public, under that service's terms.

Purchases

Becoming a Supporter is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We receive no name, card number or billing address. The App learns from Apple only whether the purchase is active, and saves that one fact (Supporter or not, and whether it is a subscription or lifetime) to your account.

How your record is protected

Data travels between the App and Firebase over encrypted connections and is encrypted where it is stored. Access rules on the database and on photo storage limit each signed-in account to its own documents and its own photos. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password if you sign in with email.

Children

Magimo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Retention and deleting your data

We keep your account and your record for as long as your account exists. To remove what exists:

  • A photo: delete it in the App. It is removed from our storage and database at the same time.
  • A visit or a note: email us and we will remove it.
  • Your whole account: email us from the address on your account and ask. We will delete your account, your record and your photos within 30 days, and tell you when it is done.
  • On-device data: delete the App. Everything it stored locally goes with it.
  • A subscription is not cancelled by deleting the App or your account. Manage it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent, and to do so without being treated differently for it. You can see your whole record in the App. To correct or remove something the App doesn't let you change, or for any other request, email us and we will respond within 30 days. You always have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Google and Apple operate globally, so your data may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. Each applies the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.
