Privacy Policy

Plot Keeper · Last updated 9 September 2026

This policy explains what Plot Keeper (the “App”) does with your information. The App is published by 1+1=2 LLC (“we”, “us”). It applies only to the Plot Keeper iPhone app.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, or any other personal detail.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device. We run no servers and receive no data about you.
  • The App asks for your location only if you tap Find graveyards near you, reads one position per search, and never stores or sends it.
  • The free version shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one. AdMob is the only third party that collects data about you.
  • Buying Lifetime Employee switches the advertising SDK off completely — it never starts, and no ad-related data leaves your device.
  • We run no analytics and no crash-reporting service of our own.

Information we do not collect

The App has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data. We operate no servers that receive your data, and we embed no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your progress locally so the game works and remembers where you left off. This data stays in the App's private storage on your iPhone and is not transmitted to us:

  • Overtime Pay, perk ranks, achievements and lifetime statistics (kills, seeds, shifts)
  • Your record on each cemetery: best time, whether it is Clean, attempts
  • Settings such as music and effect volume, haptics, screen shake and the joystick dead zone
  • Whether you have seen the first-shift card and whether Lifetime Employee has been purchased

Every arena in the App is built from cemetery boundary data bundled inside it, derived from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License). Playing a shift involves no network lookup.

Location

The App never asks for your location on its own. After your first cleared shift it shows a card offering to find cemeteries near you. Only if you accept does it request the standard iOS “while using the app” location permission. Each time you use that feature it reads a single position, compares it against the cemetery list inside the App to list the ten nearest, and then discards it. Your position is not stored and not sent anywhere.

Declining changes nothing else in the game. The feature can be turned off at any time in the App's Settings, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Plot Keeper.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one: to revive after a death, or to double a shift's payout. There are no banners and no interstitials.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK is the only third party that collects data from the App. When the SDK is active it may collect and transmit to Google:

DataUsed for
Device identifiers (such as the identifier for vendor)Serving and measuring ads, analytics, fraud prevention
Coarse location derived from your IP addressServing and measuring ads, analytics, fraud prevention
How you interact with the ads shownServing and measuring ads, analytics
Advertising data (which ads were shown)Serving and measuring ads, analytics
Crash, performance and other diagnostic data about the SDKKeeping the SDK working, analytics

This data is linked to your device by Google. The App does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, so the advertising identifier is not available to the SDK and the data is not used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. In regions where consent is required, the SDK serves limited, non-personalised ads.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchase

Lifetime Employee is a one-time purchase that removes ads for good and adds the groundskeeper uniform. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; we receive no payment details. After the purchase the advertising SDK is not started at all, so no ad-related data leaves your device. A record of the purchase is kept on your device so the App starts in the right state offline; your Apple Account remains the source of truth and Restore Purchases in Settings re-checks it.

Children

The App is rated 13+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Location: Settings → Your Shift location inside the App, or the iOS Location Services setting for Plot Keeper.
  • Ads: decline the rewarded offers, or purchase Lifetime Employee.
  • Data on your device: Settings → Reset save erases all progress; deleting the App removes everything.
