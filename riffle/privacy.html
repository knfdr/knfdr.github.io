Privacy Policy

Riffle · Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy explains what Riffle (the “App”) does with your information. The App is published by 1+1=2 LLC (“we”, “us”). It applies only to the Riffle iOS app.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, or any other personal detail.
  • To run the daily grid, the App signs in anonymously to our game server. Your guesses, scores, streak, hints and whether you own Riffle Plus are stored there against a random id, not against you.
  • Hints are optional. Without Plus, each hint comes after a rewarded ad from Google AdMob. AdMob is the only third party that collects data for advertising.
  • If you never use a hint, or you own Riffle Plus, the advertising SDK never starts.
  • The App sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, never who you are.
  • Saving a tape to Apple Music happens on your iPhone, with your permission. We never see your library.

Information we do not collect

The App has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or precise or approximate location. We embed no crash-reporting SDK.

The game server

Riffle's grids, scoring and rarity are worked out on our server, hosted by Supabase. When you first open the App, it creates an anonymous sign-in: a random id that lives on your device and is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account. Against that id the server stores:

  • Each guess you make and its score, and the grids you finish or give up
  • Your streak, worked out from the days you played
  • The hints you use (up to three per grid)
  • Whether you own Riffle Plus, checked against the App Store transaction Apple gives the App

Guesses also feed the anonymous answer counts that decide how rare each answer is. Nobody else can see your guesses, and we use this data only to run the game.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (sound, haptics, autoplay, the daily reminder and its time, the app icon), whether you have seen the introduction, and the anonymous sign-in stay in the App's private storage on your iPhone. The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your iPhone; nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Apple Music

Song previews, album art and song links come from Apple Music. The App asks for access to Apple Music only when you tap Save tape. With your permission it creates a playlist of your tape’s songs in your library. That happens on your iPhone through Apple’s MusicKit; we never see your library, your listening, or your Apple Account. The Spotify button opens a Spotify search for the song; the App sends Spotify nothing else.

Anonymous usage statistics

The App reports anonymous facts about how it is used to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built to avoid collecting personal data, run by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany. Examples: that a grid was started or finished and its score range, that a hint was used, that the paywall was shown or Plus was bought. No artist names you typed, no ids, no free text.

TelemetryDeck’s software adds basic technical details (the App’s version, iOS version, device model, language and region settings) and a code made by hashing the identifier iOS assigns to our apps on your device, so people can be counted without being known. It is not your advertising identifier. These statistics are not linked to you, not combined with data from other companies, and not used for advertising. TelemetryDeck’s handling of data is described in its privacy policy.

Advertising

Riffle’s only ads are optional: a rewarded ad from Google AdMob that you can choose to watch to unlock a hint. The daily grid itself is never ad-gated, and nothing interrupts play. The ads SDK starts the first time you tap a hint, not before; players who never use hints, and Riffle Plus owners, never load it.

To serve those ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects information directly from your device. We do not receive it; it goes to Google. It typically includes:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), if you allow tracking, or a non-personalized equivalent if you do not
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language, and coarse region
  • Your IP address
  • Ad events: which ads were shown, and whether you interacted with them

Google uses this to select and measure ads, cap how often you see the same one, and detect fraud. How Google handles it is governed by Google’s Privacy & Terms; see also AdMob’s disclosures. The App also takes part in Apple’s SKAdNetwork, which measures ad campaigns without identifying you.

Your choices about ads

  • App Tracking Transparency. Before any tracking identifier is used, iOS asks your permission, at your first hint. Choosing Ask App Not to Track withholds the advertising identifier; ads are then non-personalized. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Where required, a Google-provided consent form appears before any ad loads. You can change your choice later from Ad privacy choices in the App’s Settings.
  • Riffle Plus. Plus owners get hints with no ads: the advertising SDK never starts, and nothing is sent to Google.
  • Not using hints. Hints are optional. If you never tap one, no ad is ever requested.

In-app purchases

Riffle Plus is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The App sends the signed transaction Apple provides to our server so your Plus status works across reinstalls; it contains no payment card, billing address, or Apple Account details. Purchases are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act, personalized advertising can count as “sharing” or a “sale” of personal information. Declining tracking when iOS asks, not using hints, or owning Riffle Plus stops that.

Children

Riffle is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Retention and deleting your data

  • Game data: kept while the game runs, against the anonymous id only. Because it isn’t linked to you, we have no way to pick out one person’s records; deleting the App removes the id from your device, after which nothing connects that data to you.
  • On-device data: delete the App.
  • Advertising data: reset or disable the advertising identifier in Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising, and see Google’s policy for the data Google holds.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising, without being treated differently for it. Write to us and we will help, and you always have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Supabase, Google and Apple operate globally, so data may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. TelemetryDeck is based in Germany. Each applies the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.
