Privacy Policy

Sleight of Haunt · Last updated 19 October 2026

This policy explains what Sleight of Haunt (the “App”) does with your information. The App is published by 1+1=2 LLC (“we”, “us”). It applies only to the Sleight of Haunt iOS app.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, or any other personal detail.
  • Your night in progress, your progress and your settings are stored on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, your unlocks sync through your own Apple Account. We cannot see it.
  • If you are signed in to Game Center, your daily séance scores go to Apple’s Game Center.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and nothing in the App tracks you.
  • We run no servers and no crash-reporting service, and receive nothing from the App.

Information we do not collect

The App has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile of ours. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or precise or approximate location. We operate no servers of our own and embed no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Nothing about how you play is sent to us.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game locally so it works and remembers where you left off. This data stays in the App’s private storage on your iPhone, and none of it is sent to us:

  • A night in progress, and today’s daily séance in progress, so Continue picks them up exactly where you left them
  • Your progress: the ghosts you have unlocked, the Restlessness levels you have won, the lessons and tips you have already seen, and the ghost you picked last
  • Your history: the nights you have played and how they ended, and your daily séance results
  • Settings, such as sound, music and haptics
  • Whether you own the full game, and any daily scores waiting to be posted to Game Center

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the App keeps your progress (the unlocks, Restlessness levels and lessons seen above, but not a night in progress, your history or your settings) in Apple’s iCloud key-value store, so it follows you to your other devices and survives reinstalling the App.

This data lives in your private iCloud storage, under your Apple Account. It is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it, no ability to read it, and no copy of it.

Sync is entirely optional. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn iCloud off for Sleight of Haunt in iOS Settings, the App works exactly the same way and keeps everything on the one device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App posts the score of each day’s daily séance to its daily leaderboard. Game Center is Apple’s service: it shows your scores to you and, depending on your Game Center settings, to other players, under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center name or account. If you are not signed in, the App plays exactly the same.

In-app purchase

The full game is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and it can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. Apple processes the payment and tells the App only whether you own it. We never see your payment card, billing address, or Apple Account details. Purchases are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. Use Restore purchase in the App’s Settings to bring a purchase to a new device.

Children

Sleight of Haunt is made for a general audience. It has no accounts, no chat and no ads, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Retention and deleting your data

We hold no data from the App. To remove what exists:

  • On-device data: delete the App. Everything it stored locally goes with it.
  • iCloud data: in iOS Settings, open your Apple Account › iCloud, and turn off or remove Sleight of Haunt’s data. Deleting the App on all your devices and signing out of iCloud also clears it.
  • Game Center data: managed by Apple, in Settings › Game Center and at privacy.apple.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent, without being treated differently for it. Since we hold no personal information ourselves, most requests are best directed to Apple, whose data the request actually concerns. We are glad to help you work out how, and you always have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Apple operates globally, so data it handles may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.
