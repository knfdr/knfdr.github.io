Privacy Policy

Slingby · Last updated 20 October 2026

This policy explains what Slingby (the “App”) does with your information. The App is published by 1+1=2 LLC (“we”, “us”). It applies only to the Slingby iPhone app.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, or any other personal detail.
  • Your scores, progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress syncs through your own Apple Account. We cannot see it.
  • If you are signed in to Game Center, your Endless and Daily Sling scores and your achievements go to Apple’s Game Center.
  • Without Slingby Plus, you can choose to watch a short video ad from Google AdMob to keep going after a run. AdMob is the only third party that collects data about you, and it does not track you across other companies’ apps.
  • With Slingby Plus, the advertising SDK never starts, and no ad-related data leaves your device.
  • We run no servers, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and receive nothing from the App.

Information we do not collect

The App has no sign-up, no login, and no user profile of ours. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or precise location. We operate no servers of our own and embed no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Nothing about how you play is sent to us.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game locally so it works and remembers where you left off. This data stays in the App’s private storage on your iPhone, and none of it is sent to us:

  • Your best scores, best chains and runs played in Endless and the Daily Sling, and your lifetime planets, perfects and runs
  • Each Daily Sling day you played and its best score, for your streak
  • The stars you have earned on each Constellations route
  • The trails, dot styles and sky themes you have chosen
  • Settings, such as sound, music, haptics, reduce motion, high-contrast sky and larger dot
  • Whether you own Slingby Plus, and any Game Center scores waiting to be posted

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, the App keeps your progress (the scores, streak, route stars and choices above, but not your settings) in Apple’s iCloud key-value store, so it follows you to your other devices and survives reinstalling the App.

This data lives in your private iCloud storage, under your Apple Account. It is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it, no ability to read it, and no copy of it.

Sync is entirely optional. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn iCloud off for Slingby in iOS Settings, the App works exactly the same way and keeps everything on the one device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the App posts your Endless scores to its all-time and weekly leaderboards, your Daily Sling scores to the day’s leaderboard, and the achievements you earn. A score from a run you continued carries a small mark. The App also reads these leaderboards to show your place and your friends’ scores. Game Center is Apple’s service: it shows your scores to you and, depending on your Game Center settings, to other players, under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center name or account. If you are not signed in, the App plays exactly the same.

The Daily Sling’s clock

Everyone plays the same Daily Sling sky each day. To know which day it is, the App reads the current time from Apple’s website (apple.com) now and then. The request carries nothing about you or your game; like any web request, it reaches Apple from your device’s IP address.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Without Slingby Plus, the App offers one rewarded video ad per run, through Google AdMob: when a run of 20 or more ends, you can choose to watch it to keep going from your last planet. The ad plays only if you choose it. There are no banners and no interstitials.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK is the only third party that collects data from the App. When the SDK is active it may collect and transmit to Google:

Where the law requires your consent, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states, the App shows Google’s consent message before any ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time under Privacy choices in the App’s Settings. If you decline, the App serves only limited, non-personalised ads, or none.

How Google uses this information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchase

Slingby Plus is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the App only whether you own it. We never see your payment card, billing address, or Apple Account details. Purchases are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. With Slingby Plus, the advertising SDK is not started at all, so no ad-related data leaves your device. A record of the purchase is kept on your device so the App starts in the right state offline; your Apple Account remains the source of truth, and Restore purchase in the App’s Settings re-checks it.

Sharing a run

When you tap Share, the App draws an image of your run on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, and nothing is sent to us.

Children

Slingby is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no chat, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Retention and deleting your data

We hold no data from the App. To remove what exists:

  • On-device data: delete the App. Everything it stored locally goes with it.
  • iCloud data: in iOS Settings, open your Apple Account › iCloud, and turn off or remove Slingby’s data. Deleting the App on all your devices and signing out of iCloud also clears it.
  • Game Center data: managed by Apple, in Settings › Game Center and at privacy.apple.com.
  • Advertising data: held by Google; see Google’s Privacy Policy for your choices. Slingby Plus stops it altogether.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent, without being treated differently for it. Since we hold no personal information ourselves, most requests are best directed to Apple or Google, whose data the request actually concerns. We are glad to help you work out how, and you always have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Apple and Google operate globally, so data they handle may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their own privacy policies.
